Net Buddy Client — Guide

by N6WWW · what the client does, starting from what you know in NetLogger

Thank you, NetLogger. Net Buddy is only possible because of AC0ZG and the team at NetLogger.org, and the free service they give the amateur radio community. Please visit them and support their work.

You already know NetLogger. This guide starts there. Each row names a job, says how NetLogger does it, and how the Net Buddy Client does it.

Every row opens. Click it, or Tab to it and press Enter, to see the steps. Each open row has a Copy a link to this button, so you can send someone straight to the answer.

The NetLogger column comes from NetLogger’s own Help (version 3.1.7).

Award tracking in the dashboard, logbook sync setup, and Companion mode (Net Buddy running beside NetLogger) are in the Net Buddy User Guide.

Part 1 — For everyone

Anyone who checks into a net. Net control and loggers: this part is yours too.

Before the net

JobIn NetLoggerIn Net Buddy Client
Finding & Joining a net Headline In NetLogger: Select Net opens the Nets In Progress window. Pick a line, click Monitor Net. In Net Buddy Client: The list of live nets is the first screen, the nets Net Buddy tracks at the top. Open one and you are on it.

In Net Buddy Client (NBC):

  1. Start the client. The live nets are listed.
  2. Click a net, or move with the arrow keys and press Enter.
  3. The client joins the net for you. You appear in everyone’s monitor window, and you can send. The button in the header says on net.
  • Would you rather look before you join? See Auto Join or Auto Browse.
  • Each net shows its frequency, band, mode, net control, and how many are checked in.
  • AWARDS marks a net Net Buddy tracks awards for. JOINED marks one you are already on.
  • The list refreshes itself every 20 seconds, and says when the next refresh lands.
View a net after leaving Everyday Not in NetLogger In NetLogger: Stop Monitoring leaves the net, and you stop seeing it. In Net Buddy Client: Leave the net and keep watching. You drop off the net’s monitor list, but still see everything.

In Net Buddy Client (NBC):

  1. Click the on net button in the header. You have left the net.
  2. You are no longer on the net’s monitor list, but the check-in list and the chat keep updating. You can watch all the activity; you just cannot take part.
  3. Click join to come back.
Auto Join or Auto Browse Deep cut Not in NetLogger In NetLogger: Monitoring a net always lists you in its Monitors window. In Net Buddy Client: You choose what opening a net does: join it, or just browse it without being seen.

In Net Buddy Client (NBC):

  1. Tools → Settings… → Client Config.
  2. Find Join a net automatically when I open it.
    • Ticked — Auto Join (the default). Opening a net joins it.
    • Unticked — Auto Browse. Opening a net only shows it to you. You are not in anyone’s monitor window and you cannot send. Press join when you are ready.
  3. Save.
Joining several nets, and switching between them Everyday In NetLogger: File → New Net Window opens another net in a window of its own. In Net Buddy Client: Join as many nets as you like, and switch between them from one list. Each one stays joined.

In Net Buddy Client (NBC):

  1. Net Specific → Switch net…
  2. Pick a net. The client shows it. With Auto Join on, a net you open is joined too.
  • Every net you have joined carries a JOINED tag, in this list and on the start screen.
  • Switching away does not leave a net. You stay on its monitor list until you leave it — see View a net after leaving.
  • Picking the net you are already on just closes the list.
Browse previous or closed nets Everyday In NetLogger: View → Previous Nets. Choose a server, Get Previous Nets, then Load Checkins. In Net Buddy Client: One list across every server at once, with a search box.

In Net Buddy Client (NBC):

  1. File → Previous nets…
  2. Type part of the net’s name to narrow the list.
  3. Open one. Its check-in list appears, read-only.

This works between nets, when nothing is live.

Checking in

JobIn NetLoggerIn Net Buddy Client
Telling the NCS you’re checking in, out, or unavailable Headline Not in NetLogger In NetLogger: Type a message, and hope it is understood on the net. In Net Buddy Client: Custom action buttons, designed so NCS and loggers understand them and act on them the same way every time.

In Net Buddy Client (NBC):

The buttons sit under the search box at the top of the AIM pane. Each one has one letter underlined: that is its Alt key.

  • Alt+I CI — check in
  • Alt+O CO — check out
  • Alt+U UA — unavailable
  • Alt+K BK — back
  • Alt+L LC — ask for a last call
  • Alt+W WW — working
  • Alt+B BTN — back to net
  • Alt+C C* — your own message. Right-click C* to write it.

The Alt keys work even while you are typing a message. A button rests for 5 seconds after you press it, so one message cannot go out twice.

You must have joined the net to send.

Finding your own line Everyday In NetLogger: Your own row is colored teal. In Net Buddy Client: The ME chip shows your line number and your status. Press it to jump there.

In Net Buddy Client (NBC):

  • ME sits in the row above the check-in list.
  • It is white when you are available, and wears the color of your role or status otherwise.
  • It reads -- until you are on the list.

During the net: the check-in list

JobIn NetLoggerIn Net Buddy Client
Following whose turn it is Headline Not in NetLogger In NetLogger: The pink row shows whose turn it is. You scroll to find it. In Net Buddy Client: Shows the pink line too — and tick one box to AUTO-FOLLOW it. No scrolling: it stays centered on your screen.

In Net Buddy Client (NBC):

  • Tick the box inside the PINK chip, above the check-in list. The list follows the pink line every time it moves, and keeps it centered on your screen.
  • Untick it to scroll freely. Pressing PINK then jumps to the pink line once.
  • The chip also shows the pink line’s line number.
Search the check-in list Headline Not in NetLogger In NetLogger: Ctrl+F finds a call sign, and it is case-sensitive. In Net Buddy Client: Type in the find box and find any check-in by name, call sign, remarks, or award status!

In Net Buddy Client (NBC):

Type in the find box above the list. It searches call sign, name, state, county, city, member number, grid, remarks and award tags, and narrows the list as you type.

  • Capitals do not matter: w1aw finds W1AW.
  • & means AND: ohio & army finds Army members in Ohio.
  • | means OR: ohio | texas.
  • A space is just a space, so air force finds Air Force.

An orange bar over the list says when something is hidden. Click it, or press Escape in the list, to get the whole net back.

Showing only stations you haven’t worked Headline Not in NetLogger In NetLogger: Sort by status, and the stations without a (w) end up at the bottom. In Net Buddy Client: Click show all at the top of the check-in list to show ONLY the stations you haven’t worked! Escape takes you straight back to the full list.

In Net Buddy Client (NBC):

  1. Click show all, beside the find box at the top of the check-in list. It now reads not worked.
  2. The list keeps only stations you have not worked and can still call.
  3. Press Escape in the list to clear it, fast, back to the full list.

Hidden: stations you have worked, yourself, and anyone checked out or not responding. An orange bar over the list reminds you it is filtered. Clicking it also puts everyone back.

Which stations advance your awards Headline Not in NetLogger In NetLogger: No. In Net Buddy Client: A ranked list of who on this net moves which of your awards forward, your favorites first.

In Net Buddy Client (NBC):

  1. Click awards above the check-in list, or Awards → Award ranking.
  2. Stations are grouped PRIORITY, CLOSE and OTHER.
  3. Double-click a station to jump to its line.
  • A station shown in bright yellow with ! would complete an award.
  • Your priority awards are the favorites you pick in the dashboard. See the User Guide.
Seeing everything about a station Everyday In NetLogger: Columns across the row. You can hide, show and reorder them. In Net Buddy Client: Click the + or press the right-arrow key to open the row and see the rest: full name, city, county, grid, remarks, award and QSL details.

In Net Buddy Client (NBC):

  • Click the + at the start of a row, or press →. ← closes it.
  • Open as many rows as you like.
  • Three fast taps of → open every row. Three fast taps of ← close them all.
Reading a row at a glance Everyday Not in NetLogger In NetLogger: Row colors show status. View → Color Legend lists them. In Net Buddy Client: The same line colors as NetLogger — but it also shows clearly which stations you haven’t worked, which the other colors otherwise hide!

In Net Buddy Client (NBC):

  • Rows use NetLogger’s own color scheme, so nothing needs relearning.
  • A station you haven’t worked is marked on its row even when a role or status color covers it.
  • Checked-out and not-responding rows are struck through.
  • Badges on the row spell out role, worked or not, and signal reports.
  • The dot beside the line number says whether that station is on monitor. Shape tells you as well as color:
    • green circle: on monitor now
    • amber square: was here, and has gone
    • slate circle: not seen on monitor
Marking stations for yourself Everyday In NetLogger: F4 Needed (green). F6 Not Heard (yellow). Only you see them. In Net Buddy Client: The same keys, the same meaning. Only you see them, and no logger role is needed.

In Net Buddy Client (NBC):

  • Select a row. F4 marks it green: a station you mean to work.
  • F6 marks it yellow: a station you cannot hear.
  • Press the key again to clear it.

NetLogger’s F3 (Worked), F9 (clear all) and F10 (Needed Next) are not in the client: F3 is TURN there, and F9 and F10 are not yet assigned. See Keys that work differently.

Keys that work differently from NetLogger Headline In NetLogger: F2 switches the highlighter mode. F3 marks Worked. F9 clears statuses. F10 Needed Next. In Net Buddy Client: F2 moves the pink line. F3 is TURN. F9 and F10 are not yet assigned.

NetLogger and Net Buddy Client (NBC), key by key:

The same in both: F4 green, F5 checked out, F6 yellow, F7 unavailable, F8 no response.

Different. In the client, F2 and F3 — with or without Shift — are net control and logger keys.

  • F2 — NetLogger: manual or automatic highlighter. NBC: move the pink line here, without telling the net.
  • Shift+F2 — NetLogger: not in its key list. NBC: move the pink line here and announce their turn. TURN carries on from here.
  • F3 — NetLogger: mark Worked. NBC: TURN — advance the net to the next station and announce it.
  • Shift+F3 — NetLogger: not in its key list. NBC: jump here and announce. The next TURN goes back to where the list was.
  • F9 — NetLogger: clear all statuses. NBC: not yet assigned.
  • F10 — NetLogger: Needed Next. NBC: not yet assigned.
  • Ctrl+F — NetLogger: find a call sign. Client: use the find box.
  • Ctrl+Q — NetLogger: QRZ page. Client: right-click the row, Look up on QRZ.

Press ? in the client for every key it has.

Looking a station up Everyday In NetLogger: Ctrl+Q opens their QRZ page. In Net Buddy Client: Right-click any line, or any AIM message, for a station report, their check-in history, or QRZ.

In Net Buddy Client (NBC):

  • Station report — from your own log: have you worked them, on which bands, and what is still needed.
  • Check-in history — from omiss.net: how often they are on. OMISS nets only.
  • Look up on QRZ — opens their QRZ page in your browser.

Someone not on screen? Press Ctrl+D then S (report) or H (history), and type a line number, call sign or member number. The same two are in the Tools menu.

Sorting the list Deep cut In NetLogger: Click a column header. Click # to go back. In Net Buddy Client: A sort menu, including sort by status.

In Net Buddy Client (NBC):

  • Choose Line #, Callsign, State, Name, Remarks, Member ID or Status.
  • Status groups the list the way it is colored: checked out, unavailable, not responding, roles, worked, then everyone still to work.
  • Line # is the net’s own order, the numbers called on the air. Escape in the list goes back to it.

During the net: chat and people

JobIn NetLoggerIn Net Buddy Client
The net’s chat (AIM) Everyday Not in NetLogger In NetLogger: The AIM window. Net control’s messages and yours are colored. BB codes and emoticons work. In Net Buddy Client: The same chat, plus what NetLogger lacks: new messages on a different background, a banner when unread messages arrive while you’re scrolled up, NCS, logger and relay names all color-coded — not just NCS — and many more emoji.

In Net Buddy Client (NBC):

  • The sender’s name takes their role color: blue net control, purple logger, orange relay, yellow everyone else. Net control’s whole message is colored, so instructions are easy to find.
  • Messages from the latest refresh have a different background.
  • Scrolled up reading? When new messages arrive, an orange banner tells you there are unread messages below. Click it to go to them.
  • Many more emoji — several times NetLogger’s eight, in the chat and in private messages, with more on the way. Typed emoticons like :) turn into emoji, and the emoji button above the typing box shows them all to pick from.
  • BB codes like [b]bold[/b] show as real formatting.
  • Links open in your normal web browser.
  • The A− and A+ in the AIM pane change only the chat’s text size.
  • Drag the edge between the list and the chat to make the chat wider. Double-click the edge to reset it.
Searching the chat Headline Not in NetLogger In NetLogger: No. You scroll. In Net Buddy Client: A find box at the top of the AIM pane shows only the messages that contain a word or call sign.

In Net Buddy Client (NBC):

  1. Type in the find box at the very top of the AIM pane.
  2. Clear it to see everything again.

If new messages arrive while the search hides them, an orange bar says how many. Click it to clear the search.

Saying 73, fast Everyday Not in NetLogger In NetLogger: You type each one. In Net Buddy Client: Goodbye Mode: Ctrl+G lets you choose who to say goodbye to — OR right-click their message and say 73 from there!

In Net Buddy Client (NBC):

Goodbye Mode — for a run of 73s, one key each:

  1. Ctrl+G. An orange 73 band appears on the AIM pane.
  2. ↑ ↓ to pick a station by the message they sent.
  3. G says 73 and stays for the next one. Enter says 73 and leaves.
  4. Escape leaves without sending.

Just one station? Right-click their message and choose Say 73.

No logger role needed.

Private chat with other hams Headline Not in NetLogger In NetLogger: Not possible. In Net Buddy Client: The PEOPLE list shows who is running Net Buddy, here and on other nets. Double-click anyone to chat privately.

In Net Buddy Client (NBC):

  • NET is the people on this net with you. GLOBAL is everyone else, with the net they are on.
  • Double-click a name to open a private chat window. + Add brings in a third station.
  • The same emoji as the net’s chat work here, with an emoji button to pick from.
  • A chime plays, and a collapsed PEOPLE bar flashes, when a private message is waiting.
  • Escape in a chat window minimizes it. The × ends the conversation.
  • LURKERS, at the bottom, are on monitor for this net but not checked in. They come from NetLogger, so you cannot message them.
  • Click the word PEOPLE to fold the list away. Net Specific → People and games brings it back.
  • If it goes quiet and you think it should not, right-click the PEOPLE bar to reconnect.
Moving between the list and the chat, no mouse Deep cut Not in NetLogger In NetLogger: No. In Net Buddy Client: In the check-in list, A takes you to the AIM chat. In AIM, the up or down arrow takes you back to the list — to log a QSO fast!

In Net Buddy Client (NBC):

  • In the check-in list, press A and you are in the AIM message box. Any letter works, and it becomes the first letter of your message; A is the easy one to remember.
  • In the message box, ↑ or ↓ takes you back to the check-in list, one row moved.
  • Then Enter on the row logs the QSO.
Ignoring a station on AIM Deep cut In NetLogger: Monitors window, click the station, AIM Ignore. Loggers can ignore for everyone. In Net Buddy Client: Not in the client.

In Net Buddy Client (NBC):

Not available. Use NetLogger for this.

Games (beta) Deep cut Not in NetLogger In NetLogger: No. In Net Buddy Client: Start a game table or join one, with other Net Buddy users.

In Net Buddy Client (NBC):

  • GAMES is on the PEOPLE rail. Choose a game and press Start.
  • Double-click an open table to join it. Leave closes your own table.

During the net: your radio and your log

JobIn NetLoggerIn Net Buddy Client
CAT Control: Auto-QSY to net freq Headline Not in NetLogger In NetLogger: No rig control. In Net Buddy Client: Join a net and your radio moves to its frequency by itself, via Hamlib/rigctld, Omni-Rig, or ACLog TCP. A QSY button takes you back if you tune away.

In Net Buddy Client (NBC):

  • Set it up once: Tools → Settings… → Rig control, and choose how Net Buddy reaches your radio — Hamlib/rigctld, Omni-Rig, or ACLog (TCP). Hamlib and Omni-Rig work with any logger, or none. ACLog needs ACLog running with your rig connected. Step by step: the User Guide.
  • It moves the radio for nets you have joined. Just browsing a net does not touch your radio.
  • It will not drag you back while you are tuned off working someone.
  • When your radio is off the net’s frequency, a QSY button appears in the header. Press it to go back. It goes away once the radio is there.
  • The client reads the radio back before it tells you anything: confirmed, refused and where the radio actually is, or sent but not confirmed.
  • If the move fails — your logging program was not running, say — it tries again the next time you come back to that net.
Logging a contact Everyday In NetLogger: Select the row, click Log Contact. Usually you only type the signal reports. In Net Buddy Client: Select the row and press Enter. The Log QSO window opens already filled in.

In Net Buddy Client (NBC):

  1. Select the station’s row.
  2. Press Enter, or double-click the row.
  3. The Log QSO window opens, already filled in. Check the details and add the reports.
  4. Press Enter, or click Log QSO, to save it.
Noting a signal report before you log Deep cut In NetLogger: Type it in the RST Sent column on the station’s row. In Net Buddy Client: R notes the report you are giving a station, without logging. It is filled in for you when you log.

In Net Buddy Client (NBC):

  1. Select the row and press R.
  2. Type the report and press Enter. Escape throws it away.

It stays with that line for the rest of the net. Nothing is sent to the net.

Auto-logging combo station blocks Everyday Not in NetLogger In NetLogger: The logger can mark stations CMB. You log each station separately. In Net Buddy Client: Work any one station in a combo block, and NBC offers to log a contact with every adjacent station that has COMBO in its remarks — in one step.

In Net Buddy Client (NBC):

  1. Log the contact with one station in the combo, as usual.
  2. If the lines around it have the word combo in Remarks, the client lists them, with their remarks, and asks who else you worked.
  3. Tick the stations and press Log. Escape logs only the first one.

Band, mode, time and signal report are shared. Call sign, member number and location stay each station’s own.

Your own contact log Everyday In NetLogger: The Contacts table under the check-ins, with filters for this call, this net and more. In Net Buddy Client: A contact log along the bottom that you can open, resize, sort and edit.

In Net Buddy Client (NBC):

  • Open it with ▸ contact log at the bottom left, File → Contact log, or L in the list.
  • Drag its top edge to make it taller. It remembers whether you left it open.
  • all nets and only (this call) narrow what it shows. sort orders it.
  • Enter or double-click edits a contact. Right-click for Edit, Delete, New and Refresh.
  • Delete asks first, and makes a backup before it changes anything.
  • L again takes you back to the check-in list.

After the net

JobIn NetLoggerIn Net Buddy Client
Saving the check-in list Everyday In NetLogger: File → Save Check-ins, in NetLogger’s own format. Auto Save can do it for you. In Net Buddy Client: Export it as a spreadsheet (CSV) or as ADIF.

In Net Buddy Client (NBC):

  • File → Export check-in list (CSV)… — the list as it stood, in line order.
  • File → Export check-in list (ADIF)… — the same rows as records a logging program can read. The file says plainly that a check-in is not a worked contact.

The file goes in an exports folder beside your Net Buddy data, and the client tells you where.

Exporting your log Everyday In NetLogger: File → Export Contacts, ADIF or CSV. CSV carries only some fields. In Net Buddy Client: ADIF that keeps every field, or CSV, for the whole log or a date range.

In Net Buddy Client (NBC):

  1. File → Export my contact log…
  2. Choose ADIF (to move your log) or CSV (to read and count it).
  3. Leave the dates empty for everything, or set a range — one month for an award application, say.

Dates are UTC, like your log, and both ends of the range are included.

Sending contacts to your logbook Everyday In NetLogger: Upload to LoTW and eQSL from the eLogs menu. In Net Buddy Client: Sends new contacts to ACLog, N3FJP, Ham Radio Deluxe, QRZ, K1ALF, and loggers that listen for WSJT-X. Not LoTW or eQSL.

In Net Buddy Client (NBC):

  • Set each one up in Tools → Settings…. Each has a Test button.
  • Step-by-step setup is in the User Guide.

Nothing to do. It comes back the way you left it.

Supporting NetLogger and Net Buddy Deep cut In NetLogger: A donate button on netlogger.org keeps their servers running. In Net Buddy Client: Help → Support NetLogger and Net Buddy… — NetLogger first.

Part 2 — For net control and loggers

Everything in Part 1 applies to you too. This part is the extra job.

Most of it needs the logger role on the net. Without it, the client tells you so in words instead of failing quietly.

Taking the logger’s seat

JobIn NetLoggerIn Net Buddy Client
Becoming the logger Headline In NetLogger: Start Logging with the net password, or be promoted from the Monitors window. In Net Buddy Client: Be promoted by the current logger. The client notices by itself.

In Net Buddy Client (NBC):

  1. Join the net.
  2. Ask the current logger to promote you. In NetLogger that is the Monitors window, Promote Logger.
  3. When the promotion arrives, the ME chip turns bright green and the TURN button appears.
What still needs NetLogger Everyday In NetLogger: Create Net, Close Net, promoting and demoting loggers, and ignoring a station for everyone. In Net Buddy Client: Not in the client yet. Use NetLogger for these.

In Net Buddy Client (NBC):

Not available yet. Use NetLogger for:

  • Creating a net, and closing it at the end.
  • Promoting another station to logger, and taking it away again (Promote Logger and Demote, in NetLogger’s Monitors window).
  • AIM Group Ignore.

Opening the net

JobIn NetLoggerIn Net Buddy Client
Announcements Headline Not in NetLogger In NetLogger: You type or paste each one into AIM. In Net Buddy Client: Ten buttons you write once. Alt+1 to Alt+0 send them.

In Net Buddy Client (NBC):

  1. Click announce above the check-in list, or Net Specific → Announcements.
  2. Right-click a button to write its name and its message.
  3. Press the button, or Alt and its number. The number row and the number pad both work.
  • Each button shows its number in the corner.
  • + adds more buttons after the first ten. Those have no key.
  • A button rests for 5 seconds after you press it.
  • Anyone who has joined can send announcements. You do not need the logger role.

Checking people in

JobIn NetLoggerIn Net Buddy Client
Typing check-ins in Headline In NetLogger: Click the Call Sign cell and type a call or member ID. Tab and Enter look it up and move on. In Net Buddy Client: Entry mode: type a call or member number, press Enter, and you are on the next line.

In Net Buddy Client (NBC):

  1. In the check-in list, press I. A band across the list names the mode, the line you are typing into, and how to stop.
  2. Type a call sign or a member number. The client works out which, and fills the rest from the roster and the callbook.
  3. Tab moves across the line — call, m/p, state, name, remarks — and keeps what you typed. Shift+Tab or Home goes back to the call sign.
  4. Enter saves the line and moves down to the next. It is the only key that saves.
  5. ↑ or ↓ leaves entry mode and moves, without saving the line you were on. Press I to come back.
  6. Escape leaves entry mode.

Shortcuts after the call sign, the way stations sign on the air:

  • /m mobile, /p portable
  • /q puts QRP in remarks; /q10 puts QRP 10W
  • /new puts NEW in remarks; /fam puts FAM
  • They combine: 4711/p/new. Anything else after a slash stays part of the call sign, so W1AW/4 is left alone.
Check-in mode: one box, safer with two loggers Headline Not in NetLogger In NetLogger: Each logger types into a line they pick from their own screen. In Net Buddy Client: Type who, press Enter. The client reads the net again and puts them on the next empty line.

In Net Buddy Client (NBC):

  1. Press C in the check-in list. The Check-in mode box opens.
  2. Type a call sign or member number — the shortcuts like /m work here too — and press Enter.
  3. The client reads the net fresh and writes the station on the next empty line. The box clears for the next one.
  4. The box stays open and shows the last few that landed, with their line numbers.
  5. Escape when you are done.
  • If an entry is refused — already on line 14, say — the box tells you and selects what you typed, so you can fix it or type straight over it.
  • Why it is safer: typing into the list, you pick the line from a screen that can be 20 seconds old, and two loggers can pick the same line. Here the line is chosen a moment before the write. It is not a lock — two Enters in the same second can still collide — but the window is far smaller.
Fixing a line Everyday In NetLogger: Click a cell to type over it, or double-click to edit. In Net Buddy Client: E opens every field of the line in one box, and can refill it from the roster and QRZ.

In Net Buddy Client (NBC):

  1. Select the line and press E, or right-click it and choose EDIT.
  2. Change what you need: call sign, member number, name, city, state, county, grid, remarks.
  3. Rebuild fresh refills it from the call sign and shows what changed. It does not save.
  4. Save to the net.
Inserting, deleting and clearing lines Everyday In NetLogger: Right-click: Insert Row, Delete Row, Clear Row. In Net Buddy Client: One key each, and each one asks first.

In Net Buddy Client (NBC):

  • Insert — blank lines above; it asks how many. Everyone below moves down.
  • Delete — delete the line. Everyone below moves up.
  • Backspace — empty the line. Every line number stays put.

The delete box offers four choices, from just this line up to clearing the whole list. The bigger the choice, the stronger its color.

  • A big card in the middle of your screen shows who you are calling — see Big call-out.

  • Select a row and press Shift+F3. The pink line jumps there and it is announced. The next TURN goes back to where the list was.
  • Or press →#, the right-hand half of the TURN button, and type a line number:

Big call-out of who you are calling Headline Not in NetLogger In NetLogger: No. In Net Buddy Client: After TURN, a large card in the middle of your screen shows the line, call and name you are about to call — big enough to read while you reach for the mic.

In Net Buddy Client (NBC):

  • It appears on your screen when you press TURN or jump to a line.
  • White for a normal turn. Orange for a last call.
  • Underneath, it says whether that station is on monitor.
  • It takes no keys and no clicks away from you. It goes by itself after 10 seconds, or when you click it.

Pink Line Protection Headline Not in NetLogger In NetLogger: No. In Net Buddy Client: If someone moved the pink line since your last TURN, TURN stops and asks which station you meant.

In Net Buddy Client (NBC):

Moving the pink line yourself Everyday In NetLogger: In manual mode, click the row number. F2 switches manual and automatic. In Net Buddy Client: Keys to move it quietly, or move it and announce.

In Net Buddy Client (NBC):

Select a row, then:

  • F2 — move the pink line here. The net is not told. Double-clicking the line number does the same.
  • Shift+F2 — move it here and announce. TURN carries on from here.

Checked out, unavailable, no response Everyday In NetLogger: F5, F7, F8, logger only. In Net Buddy Client: The same keys, and the client says in words which way it went.

In Net Buddy Client (NBC):

  • F5 checked out. F7 unavailable. F8 no response. Press again to clear.
  • The same three are on the right-click menu.
  • The footer says, for example, “W1AW is now unavailable”, because the color changes on the next refresh.
Log mode: act on whoever just messaged Headline Not in NetLogger In NetLogger: Read the AIM, find their row, then press the key. In Net Buddy Client: Pick the station by their AIM message and press one letter. You never leave the chat.

In Net Buddy Client (NBC):

  1. Ctrl+Space. An amber LOG band appears on the AIM pane.
  2. ↑ ↓ to pick a station by the message they sent.
  3. Press a letter:
    • A unavailable, or back again
    • C checked out, or back in
    • W waiting room: check them in, marked unavailable
    • L add them to the Last Call list
    • G say 73, and stay in log mode
    • N TURN — no station needs picking
    • J jump to a line you name
  4. Escape or Ctrl+Space again leaves log mode.

Someone talking in AIM need not be checked in, so “not on the list — check them in first” is an ordinary answer, not an error.
